Beyond the papers

Books, badminton, movement—and one discerning cat.

A website should leave room for the person doing the research.

Outside work, I am mostly at home with my husband and our cat. While neither has learned to define a variety, both spend considerable time with my Springer books. My husband uses the different volumes to lift an iPad to exactly the right height while cooking; my cat prefers rubbing his chin along the hard-copy corners. Hartshorne is his favorite. Good taste.

While living in the United States, I earned teaching certifications in both yoga and Pilates and coached sessions in Westwood and Santa Monica. These days, I mostly use that training to help myself—and colleagues who spend too long at a screen—move and stretch a little better.

I have played badminton since I was young, including amateur tournaments at HKU, Stony Brook, and UCLA, mostly in mixed and women's doubles. In Dresden, I play with TSV Dresden.

I try to protect time, embrace the clarifying power of writing, and, in general, be kind.
