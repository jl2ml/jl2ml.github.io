Beyond the papers

Books, badminton, movement, and one discerning cat.

Outside research, I spend my time with family and friends, books, badminton, and movement, usually under the supervision of a cat with strong opinions about Hartshorne.

At home, I live with my husband and our cat. Both have found practical uses for my Springer books. My husband stacks them to raise an iPad to precisely the right height while cooking; our cat prefers rubbing his chin along the corners of my hard copies. Hartshorne is his favorite. I approve.

While living in the United States, I earned teaching certifications in yoga and Pilates and taught in Westwood and Santa Monica. I no longer teach regularly, but the training stays with me: I pay attention to how small adjustments change movement, and I occasionally remind colleagues who have spent one hour too many at a computer to stretch.

Badminton has been part of my life since I was young. I have competed in amateur tournaments at HKU, Stony Brook, and UCLA, mostly in mixed and women's doubles. In Dresden, I play with TSV Dresden.

I share my friend Bingbin Liu's values: protect your time, embrace the power of writing and clarity of thought, and, in general, be kind.
