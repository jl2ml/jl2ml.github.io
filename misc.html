Beyond the papers

Books, badminton, movement, and one discerning cat.

A website should leave room for the person doing the research.

Outside work, I am usually at home with my husband and our cat. Neither has learned to define a variety, but both make excellent use of my Springer books. My husband stacks them to lift an iPad to precisely the right height while cooking; our cat prefers rubbing his chin along the corners of my hard copies. Hartshorne is his favorite. Good taste.

While living in the United States, I earned teaching certifications in yoga and Pilates and coached sessions in Westwood and Santa Monica. These days, that training mostly helps me move and stretch a little better. Colleagues who have spent one hour too many at a computer sometimes benefit too.

I have played badminton since I was young, including amateur tournaments at HKU, Stony Brook, and UCLA, mostly in mixed and women's doubles. In Dresden, I play with TSV Dresden.

I share my friend Bingbin Liu's values: protect your time, embrace the power of writing and clarity of thought, and, in general, be kind.
