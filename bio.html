Jiayi Li

A concise biography and portrait for talk announcements, programs, and other professional use.

Dr. Jiayi Li is a postdoctoral researcher in the Section of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics in Dresden and an ILIAD Fellow in applied mathematics for AI alignment. Her research lies at the intersection of algebraic geometry, statistics, and theoretical machine learning. She develops mathematical tools for understanding the structure and learning dynamics of neural networks, with the long-term goal of supporting more interpretable, robust, and reliable AI. Jiayi received her PhD in Statistics from UCLA, where she was a member of the Mathematical Machine Learning Group.
