Jiayi Li

A biography, pronunciation guide, and portrait for talk announcements, programs, and other professional use.

Jiayi Li is a postdoctoral researcher in the Section of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics and an ILIAD Fellow in applied mathematics for AI alignment. Her research lies at the intersection of algebraic geometry, statistics, and theoretical machine learning. She studies how geometry, symmetry, and singularities shape what neural networks can represent, how they learn, and which properties of the resulting models are identifiable. This work aims to provide rigorous foundations for interpretability and reliable AI. Jiayi received her PhD in Statistics from UCLA's Mathematical Machine Learning Group.

Pronunciation: Jiayi Li is pronounced roughly “jyah-yee lee.”
